## Problem
Early detection of chronic co-morbidities (such as cardiovascular conditions, diabetes, and kidney diseases) is frequently hindered by siloed clinical records, non-standardized diagnostic measurements, and the black-box nature of many machine learning classifiers, limiting clinical adoption in preliminary screenings.

## Architecture
The system employs an ensemble machine learning pipeline designed to evaluate diverse clinical indicators. Raw patient biometric records undergo robust imputation, min-max scaling, and mutual-information-based feature selection. The pipeline deploys tailored ensemble classifiers (Random Forest, XGBoost, and deep neural networks) with SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) integration to provide transparent, feature-level attribution for medical practitioners.
